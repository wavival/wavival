# Roadmap

Learning repositories I publish as study notebooks for the community. All content is written in Spanish and follows a shared structure: modules with numbered lessons, examples, exercises, a sources section, and open contributions.

Status: **Active** (lessons being published), **Started** (structure ready, little content) or **Planned** (not started).

## Published

- [fundamentos-javascript](https://github.com/wavival/fundamentos-javascript): JavaScript from the language basics to Node.js, testing and security. Status: Started.
- [fundamentos-llms](https://github.com/wavival/fundamentos-llms): how large language models work, from tokens to the Transformer and GPT-2. Status: Started.
- [fundamentos-terminal-linux](https://github.com/wavival/fundamentos-terminal-linux): getting started with the Linux terminal. Status: Started.

## Planned

New topics are added here as they are defined.
